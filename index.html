02What's coming next

  • Multiplayer. The next big one: take on the skibidis with friends.
  • Enemy upgrades. Skibidis that start with guns and work up through armor, jetpacks, rockets and lasers.
  • The sound pass. A real laser sound, battle music beyond the menu track, and the skibidis singing their theme song.
  • Main menu redesign. A screenshot background, bigger rounded buttons, the new layout.
  • Save system. Your bank, best wave and characters persist, with a reset-progress option and a no-save test mode.
  • Titan shop. The Titan section plus a modular upgrade shop built on the 35 weapon models.
  • Astro secret. The secret orb and the unlock behind it.

05Music

Every track that goes into the game or this site gets a copyright check before it's used. If a track turns out to be copyrighted, it stays but it shows right here with full credit to whoever made it — and the game's settings menu gets a music toggle so you can turn all music off if you'd rather play without it.
