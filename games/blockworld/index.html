What it is

A block-building world game in the spirit of the classics: dig, build, craft and explore, with an original engine and 100% original art. It is not a mod of anyone else's game and uses no assets from other games.

In the game now

Infinite terrain

Chunked world with caves, trees and hills, streamed around you.

Build and dig

Break and place blocks with a hotbar, day and night cycle.

Monsters and beds

Difficulty from Peaceful to Hard, beds to survive the night.

Skins

Player skins, a Dressing Room and an emote wheel.

XP and enchanting

Earn experience and enchant your gear.

Sound and music

Ambient audio with a music toggle in the settings.

The Nether and the End

Portals, Nether fortresses, strongholds and the Ender Dragon.

A much taller world

Build up to y 1007, dig down to y -112, giant snowy peaks.

Game modes

Survival, Hardcore and Creative, with Keep Inventory and World Settings.

Weather, oceans and rivers

Rain, wind, lightning, waterfalls, oceans and rivers.

Mods you switch on

Guns & cannons, Skibidi Toilets, security cameras, decor and more, each with its own switch per world.

Play together

Host and join

One player hosts a world, everyone else joins it. Works PC to PC: a friend, a brother, a wife or the kids can all play in the same world.

Same Wi-Fi

On one network, joining is the easy path: host, see the world, join.

Over the internet

Yes, someone in Canada can play with someone in Texas. One side opens a port on their router, and an auto-setup helper plus a test screen are planned to make that painless.

One honest note: Blockworld is its own game with its own netcode, so players of the real Minecraft can't join. Friends just install Blockworld and play together here.

Phones and tablets

The web test has touch controls: a joystick and on-screen buttons that turn on the moment you touch the screen. Turn your phone sideways, and give the first load a minute on cell data. The world is heavy to render, so older phones may need lower settings.

Runs on
