BLOCKWORLD

Ttvm Studios' own block-building world game. Fresh code, fresh original art, and one rule above all: it has to run smooth on modest PCs first.

What it is

A block-building world game in the spirit of the classics: dig, build, craft and explore, with an original engine and 100% original art. It is not a mod of anyone else's game and uses no assets from other games.

In the game now

Infinite terrain

Chunked world with caves, trees and hills, streamed around you.

Build and dig

Break and place blocks with a hotbar, day and night cycle.

Monsters and beds

Three difficulty levels, beds to survive the night.

Skins

A dressing room is coming, and player skins are in.

XP and enchanting

Earn experience and enchant your gear.

Sound and music

Ambient audio with a music toggle in the settings.

Play together

Host and join

One player hosts a world, everyone else joins it. Works PC to PC: a friend, a brother, a wife or the kids can all play in the same world.

Same Wi-Fi

On one network, joining is the easy path: host, see the world, join.

Over the internet

Yes, someone in Canada can play with someone in Texas. One side opens a port on their router, and an auto-setup helper plus a test screen are planned to make that painless.

One honest note: Blockworld is its own game with its own netcode, so players of the real Minecraft can't join. Friends just install Blockworld and play together here.

Phones and tablets

Planned, like Skibidi Strike: a browser build with touch controls so nobody needs a computer to play. It comes after the PC version is solid; the world is heavy to render, so older phones may need reduced settings.

Runs on
